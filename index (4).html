What we do

A link that holds, from forest to port

We pair sustainable forestry with disciplined manufacturing, managing our own timberlands so the wood behind every pallet is accounted for from the day it's planted to the day it leaves our yard.

01 / Compliance

Accreditation

Every export-grade unit is heat-treated to a core temperature of at least 56°C for 30 minutes, clearing international phytosanitary standards and keeping shipments moving without delay at destination ports.

02 / Regeneration

Active Reforestation

For every tree we harvest, several more seedlings go into the ground, keeping a steady cycle of growth that pulls carbon from the air and keeps the local soil healthy.

03 / Engineering

Load Capacity

Reinforced stringers and deck boards go into every heavy-duty pallet we build, so it carries up to 1,200kg without warping, even under sustained pressure.

04 / Service

Professionalism

Doing this work well means looking past the bottom line. We hold ourselves to a standard where good work and a genuine benefit to our communities and environment go hand in hand.

Our forests, our legacy

Sustainability, the way it should actually work

Since 2013, we've run a closed-loop forestry model built on one simple rule: we never take from the land faster than it can grow back.

Responsible Sourcing

Every piece of wood we use comes from farms and forests managed with biodiversity and long-term ecosystem health in mind, not stripped for a single harvest.

Active Reforestation

For every tree we harvest, we plant several more in its place, so the forest keeps pulling carbon out of the air and holding the soil together long after we're gone.

Circular Economy

A damaged pallet gets a second life through repair, and wood that's truly reached the end of the line still finds a purpose as mulch or biofuel rather than a landfill.
